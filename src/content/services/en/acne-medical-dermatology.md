## Why DermaBay for medical dermatology

Aesthetic treatments make the headlines, but medical dermatology is the foundation of the clinic. A skin problem that is diagnosed correctly and treated early saves months of trial and error and prevents the scars and marks that aesthetic treatments later have to fix.

You get a clear explanation, a written plan and a follow-up date. If something changes between visits, you message us.

## What to bring

- A list or photos of what you've been using on your skin.
- Any previous prescriptions or test results.
- Photos of flare-ups if the problem comes and goes.
