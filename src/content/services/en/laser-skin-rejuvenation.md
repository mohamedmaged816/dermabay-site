## Why DermaBay for laser rejuvenation

This is the treatment for people who want their skin to look better without anyone knowing why. The key on Egyptian skin is choosing the right wavelength and energy so you get the collagen response without pigment problems. We patch test, start moderate and increase only as your skin proves it tolerates it.

## Before your session

- No tanning or self-tanner for 2 weeks.
- Stop retinoids and exfoliating acids for 3 days.
- Come with clean skin, no make-up or sunscreen.
