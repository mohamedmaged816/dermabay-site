## Why DermaBay for fractional laser

Fractional laser works by creating thousands of microscopic treatment columns that trigger new collagen while leaving the surrounding skin intact to heal quickly. Done well on Egyptian skin, it is one of the most reliable ways to soften acne scars. Done carelessly, it leaves pigmentation that takes months to fade. The difference is settings and patience, not the machine's name.

Our protocol starts conservative, with a patch test and lower density, then builds up only if your skin shows it heals cleanly. You see photos of your own progress at every visit.

## What to expect after

- Day 1 to 2: red, warm, tight, like a strong sunburn. Cool compresses and the barrier cream we give you.
- Day 3 to 5: bronzing and fine flaking. Do not pick. Gentle cleanser, moisturiser, SPF 50.
- Week 2 onward: fresh, slightly pink skin. Texture starts improving over the following weeks.
