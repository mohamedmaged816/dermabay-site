## Why DermaBay for pigmentation

Pigmentation is where most "quick fix" clinics fail, because the fastest treatments are the ones most likely to backfire on darker skin. Our approach is dermatology first: identify what kind of pigmentation it is, treat the trigger, then lighten it with the gentlest effective method. It takes a few months. It also lasts.

We photograph your skin under the same light at each visit, so you and we can see the change honestly.

## What to expect after an in-clinic session

- Peels: light tightness for a day, then 2 to 3 days of fine flaking.
- Low-energy laser: pink for a few hours, no peeling.
- Mesotherapy: tiny bumps for a day.
- Always: sunscreen in the morning, reapplied at midday, every single day.
