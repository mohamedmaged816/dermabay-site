## Why DermaBay for rejuvenation

Injectables are safe and natural-looking in trained medical hands, and risky everywhere else. At DermaBay the person assessing you is the person treating you, the products are traceable, and we treat conservatively because you can always add but you cannot easily take away.

You'll leave the consultation with a written plan and a fixed price, so nothing is a surprise.

## Before your session

- Avoid aspirin, ibuprofen, fish oil and alcohol for 2 to 3 days to reduce bruising, unless prescribed.
- Tell us about any medications, previous treatments and allergies.
- Come without make-up if possible.
