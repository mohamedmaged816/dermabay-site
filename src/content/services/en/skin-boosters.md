## Why DermaBay for skin boosters

The result of a skin booster depends on who holds the syringe: depth, spacing and product choice decide whether you get even hydration or lumps. Our injectors treat skin boosters as a medical procedure with a mapped injection pattern, and we only use products registered for use in Egypt.

We also tell you when a booster is not the right answer. If your real concern is volume loss or sagging, we'll say so rather than sell you a glow.

## Before your session

- Avoid aspirin, ibuprofen and fish oil for 3 days unless prescribed, to reduce bruising.
- Skip alcohol the night before.
- Come with clean skin, no make-up.
