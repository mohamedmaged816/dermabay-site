faqs:
  - q: How much hair loss is normal?
    a: Losing 50 to 100 hairs a day is normal. Clumps in the shower, a visibly wider parting or a receding line are not, and are worth checking.
  - q: What causes sudden shedding?
    a: Most often telogen effluvium, a shedding phase 2 to 3 months after a trigger such as illness, fever, surgery, crash dieting, childbirth or severe stress. It usually recovers with the right support.
  - q: Do I need blood tests?
    a: Often yes. Low iron stores, thyroid problems and low vitamin D are common and fixable causes in Egypt, and we won't guess when we can measure.
  - q: Does PRP work?
    a: PRP helps many people with pattern thinning when done in a proper series alongside medical treatment. It is not a cure and not a replacement for medication; we'll tell you if you're a good candidate.
  - q: Is hair loss different in women?
    a: Women more often have diffuse thinning and hormonal causes such as PCOS, and need a different workup. We treat women and men.
  - q: How long do I continue treatment?
    a: For pattern loss, as long as you want to keep the result. For shedding after a trigger, usually 6 to 12 months then stop.

## Why DermaBay for hair loss

Hair loss is one of the most over-sold problems in aesthetic medicine. Expensive sessions get offered before anyone has checked whether your iron is low or your thyroid is off. At DermaBay the dermatologist examines the scalp, orders tests when they matter and tells you which type of hair loss you have before suggesting a single session.

If a transplant is genuinely your best option, we'll tell you that too.

## Before your visit

- Don't wash your hair the morning of the visit; a day's natural shedding helps the examination.
- Bring any previous blood results and a list of supplements and medications.
- If you can, bring photos from 6 to 12 months ago for comparison.
