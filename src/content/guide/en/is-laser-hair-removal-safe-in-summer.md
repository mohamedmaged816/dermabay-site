## The short answer

Yes. Laser hair removal is safe in summer. The seasonal warning you've heard is really about **tanning**, not temperature.

## Why a tan is the problem

Lasers for hair removal target melanin, the pigment in the hair root. Your skin also contains melanin. The device settings are chosen so the hair absorbs much more energy than the surrounding skin. When you tan, your skin's melanin goes up, the gap shrinks, and the skin absorbs energy it shouldn't. That is what can cause burns or dark marks, and it is why clinics on the Mediterranean were historically cautious in summer.

In Cairo most of us are not sunbathing. We're walking from the car to the building. If your treated areas are not getting darker, your sessions can continue year-round.

## The two rules

1. **No tanning for 2 weeks before and 2 weeks after** each session. That includes beach days, pool days, and self-tanner.
2. **SPF 50 every morning** on treated areas that see daylight, especially the face, arms and legs. Reapply at midday if you're outside.

## Who should wait

- Anyone with a visible tan or sunburn on the area. Wait 2 to 4 weeks until it fades.
- Anyone heading to Sahel for a week within a few days of the session. Book the session for after the trip instead.
- Anyone using photosensitising medication, which we'll check at your consultation.

## What happens if you get it wrong

A tanned area treated at full settings can develop a dark patch that takes months to fade. It is avoidable: tell us about recent sun exposure, and we lower the energy or postpone. We'd rather delay a session than treat skin that isn't ready.

## Bottom line

Summer in Cairo is a fine time to start laser. In fact, if you start now you'll be mostly done by next summer.
