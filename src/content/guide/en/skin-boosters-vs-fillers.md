## Same ingredient, different job

Both skin boosters and dermal fillers are made from **hyaluronic acid**, a sugar molecule your skin already produces that holds water. The difference is how the gel is made and where it's placed.

## Skin boosters

- **What they are**: a thin, lightly cross-linked hyaluronic acid.
- **Where they go**: micro-droplets spread across the skin itself, in the dermis.
- **What they do**: pull water into the skin, improve glow, soften fine lines and crepey texture, improve elasticity.
- **What they don't do**: add volume or change shape.
- **How long**: around 6 months after the initial 2 to 3 sessions.
- **Typical areas**: cheeks, under eyes, neck, hands, around the mouth.

## Dermal fillers

- **What they are**: a thicker, more cross-linked gel that holds its shape.
- **Where they go**: deeper, under the skin or on the bone, in precise amounts.
- **What they do**: restore lost volume, lift, define, and fill deeper folds.
- **How long**: 9 to 18 months depending on area and product.
- **Typical areas**: cheeks, chin, jawline, lips, nasolabial folds, temples.

## Which one you need

| Your concern | Likely answer |
|---|---|
| Dull, dehydrated, tired-looking skin | Skin booster |
| Fine lines on cheeks or around the mouth | Skin booster |
| Crepey under-eye skin | Skin booster |
| Hollow under-eyes or cheeks | Filler |
| Deep nasolabial folds | Filler, sometimes plus booster |
| Thin or undefined lips | Filler |
| Early sagging of the jawline | Filler, often with collagen stimulators |

## Combining them

They work well together because they solve different problems. A common plan: filler to restore structure in the mid-face, then boosters two weeks later to improve the skin's surface quality. Done in the right order, the result looks like good skin rather than a treatment.

## The honest caveat

Neither is right for everyone. If your main concern is pigmentation, acne or texture from scarring, laser and medical treatments come first. A consultation that tells you what you don't need is worth as much as one that sells you something.
