## First, which scars you have

Acne scars come in three main shapes, and the shape predicts the result:

- **Rolling scars**: soft, wave-like dips. Respond very well.
- **Boxcar scars**: sharper-edged, flat-bottomed pits. Respond well.
- **Ice-pick scars**: narrow, deep holes. Respond partially; often combined with other techniques.

Red or brown marks left after pimples are not scars but discolouration, and they're treated differently and more easily.

## How fractional laser works

The laser creates thousands of microscopic columns of controlled injury in the skin while leaving the skin between them untouched. Those intact bridges heal the treated columns fast, and the healing response builds new collagen that lifts the scar floor. Each session treats a fraction of the surface, hence "fractional", and hence multiple sessions.

## Why 3 to 5 sessions

Collagen builds in layers. One session gives a visible but modest change; by the third you see real softening; the fourth and fifth refine. Sessions are 4 to 6 weeks apart to let the skin fully recover between them. The final result is judged **6 months after the last session**, because collagen keeps remodelling long after the redness is gone.

## Downtime, day by day

- **Day 0**: numbing cream, treatment, immediate heat and redness like a strong sunburn.
- **Day 1 to 2**: red, swollen, tight. Cool compresses, barrier cream, sleep slightly elevated.
- **Day 3 to 5**: bronzed look and fine flaking. Gentle cleanser, moisturiser, SPF 50. Do not pick.
- **Day 5 to 7**: flaking finishes, fresh pink skin. Mineral make-up is fine from day 3 if you need it.
- **Week 2 onward**: pinkness fades, texture begins to improve.

## Egyptian skin: the precautions that matter

Darker skin produces pigment readily when inflamed. The protocol that keeps it safe: a patch test, lower density and energy at the first session, no tanning for 2 weeks before and 4 weeks after, SPF 50 daily, and sometimes a short course of a pigment-calming cream before and after. Clinics that push high settings for a faster result are the ones whose patients come to us later for pigmentation.

## Realistic expectations

Expect **40 to 70 percent improvement** across most scar patterns after a full course. Scars become shallower and softer, skin texture evens out, and make-up sits smoothly. Ice-pick scars may need subcision or TCA techniques alongside the laser, which the consultation will map out.
